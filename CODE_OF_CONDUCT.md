# Code of conduct

OpacityBar is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly place for anyone who uses it or helps build it,
whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to Windows internals.
- Assume good intent. Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Keep issues and discussions on topic, and accept a maintainer's decision gracefully.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexual content or unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or repeatedly derailing discussions.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to everything in the OpacityBar repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and
to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

Report problems privately through [moller.studio](https://moller.studio), or to the maintainers through
GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove content, and warn, temporarily block or
permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules, depending on how serious the problem is.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
