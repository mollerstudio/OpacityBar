# Name and logo

The OpacityBar **source code** is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE): you may use, change and
share it freely.

The **OpacityBar name and logo** are not part of that license. They belong to
[moller.studio](https://moller.studio) and identify the official app, so people know a download really comes
from this project.

**You're welcome to**

- link to OpacityBar, write about it, review it, and show screenshots of it;
- say that your fork or project is "based on OpacityBar" or "a fork of OpacityBar";
- use the logo unchanged to refer to the official app (for example in an article or a list of apps).

**Please don't**

- publish a fork, modified build or repackaged version under the OpacityBar name or with its logo:
  give it its own name and icon (replace the files in `assets/`);
- use the name or logo in a way that suggests moller.studio made, approved or supports something it didn't.

Not sure? Ask through [moller.studio](https://moller.studio).
